Lightfin is a web financial-charting library with a professional-grade
charting experience — candlesticks, indicators, drawing tools, smooth pan/zoom
and live data — while staying **small, dependency-free, and easy to wire to any
data source**.

The core is framework-agnostic vanilla TypeScript, rendered on layered
`Canvas2D`. It's _bars in, chart out_: it renders and handles interaction while
your app supplies the data and trading UI. Drawings and interactive chart
settings are saved in the browser by default.

## What you get

- **Series** — 13 chart styles, including candles, Heikin Ashi, lines, areas and columns, switchable in place.
- **[Indicators](/docs/api/indicators)** — 27 built in (moving averages,
  Ichimoku, Parabolic SAR, Supertrend, Donchian Channels and more),
  overlaid or on their own resizable pane, plus a contract for your own.
- **[Drawing tools](/docs/api/annotations)** — 25 of them, with undo/redo, a
  per-drawing style toolbar, and JSON an AI can drive.
- **Live data** — attach a `DataSource` for history backfill and a live stream.
- **[Chrome](/docs/api/chrome)** — optional HUD/legend, toolbars, instrument
  metadata, exchange clock, fullscreen.
- **Price lines & event tags** — order/position lines and earnings/dividend tags.
- **React bindings** — `<LightfinChart>` and a `useChartEffect` hook.

## Not goals

Lightfin isn't a backtesting engine, a data service, or a trading/execution UI.
It provides the _hook_ a trading UI needs — it fires price/coordinate events and
lets your host render the order form.
